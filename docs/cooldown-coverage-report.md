# Cooldown Coverage Report

Select a Midnight Season 2 boss and Heroic or Mythic, then choose **Cooldown Coverage Report** and supply Warcraft Logs report URLs/codes. Every matching encounter in each report is fetched; the result has a pull selector. Changing a pull changes both the boss events and raid pressure. No scheduled boss timestamps or inferred boss cooldowns are used.

The timeline provides one pressure graph, a raid coverage strip, one compact boss track, and healer ability lanes with optional vertical pressure shading. Boss icon toggles show/hide individual abilities; hover or keyboard focus reveals their descriptions. All/None toggles are provided. Crowded boss casts share an icon badge that opens a chooser, preserving each exact timestamp. Zoom, healer filtering and estimated readiness remain available. Icons are served locally.

Click a healer cast to open a compact efficiency drawer: effective direct healing versus overhealing, a proportional green/amber bar, both amounts, and the effective percentage. Consumed shields are listed separately because their wasted potential is unknown. Efficiency is health restored divided by health restored plus overhealing, not a universal cooldown score. A short visible note identifies this as healer/pet output during the window, including other spells. The cast panel omits additional ability, timing and talent metadata. Escape closes the drawer; selected casts remain highlighted.

## Data and interpretation

- A slim death track below the pressure graph marks every recorded player death at its pull-relative time, including repeat deaths after resurrection. Nearby markers group with a count badge; hover lists names and precise times, and click opens a compact list. Markers remain visible with pressure hidden or a single healer selected. Pets and out-of-pull events are excluded. Deaths use the existing paginated event-stream service, with no first-deaths cutoff. Cache version 3 ensures fresh results include death markers.

- `who_messed_up/data/cooldown_coverage/` preserves the supplied research and its provenance: 24 healer cooldowns and 68 boss abilities across nine bosses. This is the supplied catalogue, not a claim to include every encounter-journal spell or raid defensive.
- Recorded friendly and enemy casts are queried separately and grouped by fight. A successful `cast` is used, rather than also counting `begincast`. All pages are fetched through the existing bounded event-stream service.
- Combatant talent entry IDs are translated using the versioned `talent-entry-map.json`, extracted from SimulationCraft's 12.1 game-data export. Both entry and node IDs must match; ranks and mutually excluded variants are respected. Unknown mappings fall back to nominal timing. No runtime request to an external talent database is needed.
- Active durations use a matching paired self aura for buff cooldowns when available; otherwise they are nominal research windows, clipped at pull end. Channels may be shortened by haste or interruption. Unknown duration stays unknown; instantaneous healing stays zero. Conditional Temporal Burst, Evangelism and Halo windows require their recorded talents.
- Stasis Store is preparation, with unknown healing duration and no ready estimate. Release is tracked separately (370564); only release starts the recovery estimate. Pre-pull storage can therefore still produce a release marker. Expiry without an observed release does not invent a healing event or recovery timestamp.
- Readiness is estimated. WCL does not directly expose when a cooldown becomes usable. Static talent changes are applied; dynamic recovery is flagged, and earlier observed reuse bounds the estimate. A later cast establishes availability **by** that time, not the exact prior moment it became ready.
- Unused abilities appear only when the selected talent can be established. Shared spells such as Avenging Wrath are filtered by recorded specialization, avoiding Retribution casts in Holy lanes.
- Raid pressure bins contain health damage excluding overkill plus `healabsorbed` event amounts. Defensive absorb events are not added again. Heal absorbs are plotted **when consumed**, not as the outstanding absorb pool or the time that pool was applied. Each bin includes its largest ability sources; the final partial bin uses its actual duration.
- The coverage strip counts sustained tracked windows across **all healers**, independently of visibility filters. Green intensity indicates overlapping window count, not effectiveness or raid-wide reach. Instant and unknown-duration casts use a separate hatched indicator; Stasis Store contributes no coverage. Amber intervals have pressure at or above the 75th-percentile threshold of non-zero pressure bins and no tracked window or burst marker in that bin. These are review prompts, not proof of a healing failure. Coverage edges preserve exact cast/window times within the pressure bins.
- Effectiveness measures all output from the healer and owned pets during the displayed window, including other spells and existing HoTs/shields. It does **not** claim causal attribution to the cooldown. Direct-heal overhealing is `overheal / (health restored + overheal)`; shields count only when consumed, and healing into heal absorbs is shown as raid pressure rather than credited output. Damage prevention and health redistribution are not measured, so there is no universal efficiency score. Overlap and holding a cooldown may be intentional.
- Instant casts use a labelled three-second observation window; unknown-duration effects use five seconds. These observation windows never become active coverage bars. All analysis windows are clipped at pull end. Missing healing data is reported as unavailable, not zero effectiveness. Registry cache version 2 separates pages with measurements from the initial payloads.

## Structure

`services/cooldown_catalog.py` owns research lookup and timing resolution. `services/cooldown_coverage.py` fetches and calculates pull data; `services/cooldown_effectiveness.py` indexes healing/pressure streams and adds cast context. `services/view_models/cooldown_coverage.py` defines the timeline contract; existing table payloads keep their structure. The registry adds a separate coverage report for each supported boss/difficulty, excluded from aggregate table reports. `frontend/src/components/v2/coverage/` owns presentation, `hooks/useCoverageTimeline.js` owns interaction state, and `utils/coverageTimeline.js` owns coverage segmentation and boss clustering. `ReportPageView` only dispatches to the report.

## Verification

- Backend: `.venv/Scripts/python.exe -m pytest -q`.
- Frontend: `npm run build`, `npm run test:coverage`, and focused ESLint checks. The React Testing Library/jsdom test checks pull isolation, boss icon filters, healer filtering without changing raid coverage, gap inspection, effectiveness measurements, overlay/readiness toggles, zoom, keyboard dismissal and the ability inspector. Geometry checks distinguish instant casts and Stasis preparation from sustained coverage and preserve clustered boss timestamps.
- Backend calculation tests cover pet attribution, player targets, overheal denominators, shield consumption, preceding pressure, window clipping, overlaps, estimated hold time, and missing data.
- Death marker checks cover player-only filtering, duplicate events, repeat deaths, pull boundaries and serialization. The render fixture includes real death times from two pulls; interaction checks cover hover labels, click details, pull changes and filter independence. Boss and death tracks share `useTimelineTrackWidth` and `clusterTimelineEvents` so markers regroup consistently on zoom/resize.
- Live snapshot cases: `cooldown_coverage_sentinels_mythic` and `cooldown_coverage_vashnik_mythic` in `scripts/capture_regressions.py`.
- The checked-in render fixture contains two real Entombed Sentinels pulls with healer names replaced by sample labels. Its different Shifting Protovenom times (36.205 and 36.378 seconds) ensure that the displayed pull does not mix in another pull's events.
- The effectiveness update was additionally verified against 297 measured cooldown uses across 15 Sentinels/Vashnik pulls, including consumed defensive shields.
- Additional live validation used `AZfwY8vr1DanQbch` (Vashnik kill) and `xK1bZJTLdrVhqHDg` (eight Nek'zali pulls), for 24 pulls across four reports in total. Nek'zali Ritual of Awakening varied from 166.198 to 192.833 seconds.

Six legacy regression cases (ghosts, phase damage and add damage) matched fresh snapshots from an isolated checkout of the original HEAD. Older saved baselines already differed; they were not overwritten.

Browser visual QA could not be completed in the implementation session: Computer Use stopped because it could not determine the browser URL confidently. The DOM tests exercise controls but do not substitute for visual browser QA.
